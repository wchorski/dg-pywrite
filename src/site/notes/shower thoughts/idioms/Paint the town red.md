> The expression “paint the town red” alludes to unruly behavior resulting in bloodshed, and its origin has several suggested sources. One popular tale dates back to 1837 when the Marquis of Waterford and his friends allegedly caused havoc in Melton Mowbray, England, painting buildings red. The Marquis was known for his hooliganism, and the event was well-documented.
