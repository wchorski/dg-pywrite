> Imagine finding a $100 bill on the ground. You’d feel a quick surge of joy. Now imagine losing $100 from your wallet. Which feels stronger? For most of us, the pain of loss is sharper than the pleasure of gain.
>
> This is the essence of **loss aversion**, a principle identified by psychologists Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky in their groundbreaking work on prospect theory. Their research revealed a deep asymmetry in the human mind: _losses hurt roughly twice as much as equivalent gains feel good._
> - https://everydaythought.substack.com/p/loss-aversion-why-losses-loom-larger
