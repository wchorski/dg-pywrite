First In First Out

> FIFO means "First In, First Out." It's a valuation method in which older inventory is moved out before new inventory comes in. The first goods sold are the first goods purchased. The FIFO method maintains the newest items in inventory. While this [inventory valuation](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/specific-identification-inventory-valuation-method.asp) method doesn't have to follow the actual flow of inventory through the business, a company should justify why it selected the valuation method.

> In computing and in [systems theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_theory "Systems theory"), **first in, first out** (the first in is the first out), [acronymized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym "Acronym") as **FIFO**, is a method for organizing the manipulation of a data structure (often, specifically a [data buffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_buffer "Data buffer")) where the oldest (first) entry, or "head" of the [queue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queue_\(abstract_data_type\) "Queue (abstract data type)"), is processed first.
> - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFO_(computing_and_electronics)
