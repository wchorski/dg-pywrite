> [!quote]
> To demand punishment or retribution for a person or group, especially in a ferocious or zealous manner.

angrily demand or threaten violence an angry mob baying for blood

## used in a sentence
> My co-workers have been baying for blood ever since they found out that I lied about my credentials on my résumé. Protesters immediately bayed for blood when it was revealed that the governor had been aware of the cover-up from the start. It was an innocent mistake, but my constituents are baying for blood. Am I going to have to resign?
